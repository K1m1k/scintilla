About

      

      

        

          
I'm Kim — K1m1k online. A self-taught developer who learns by building: I take an
          idea and turn it into something that actually runs, from the first line of code to a working
          product.

          
My work moves between applied AI, accessibility, distributed systems and privacy. Alongside
          the code, I write essays about how technology reshapes power and everyday life — because the
          tools we build are never neutral.

          
Whatever I make, one principle stays fixed: software should be honest about what it does.
